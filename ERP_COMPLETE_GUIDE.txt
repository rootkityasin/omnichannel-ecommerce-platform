ERP - THE COMPLETE BUSINESS OPERATIONS PLATFORM
=======================================================

What you are looking at is not just an e-commerce website. It is a full-scale ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) system built specifically for modern retail businesses — whether you sell food, clothing, or any physical product.

The short version: Every order, every Taka, every piece of stock, every staff action is tracked, protected, and visible to you in real time. You run your business; the software handles the rest.

=======================================================
SECTION 1: THE CORE BUSINESS MODULES
=======================================================

1. SMART DASHBOARD
The moment you open the app, you see your business.
- Exactly how much money came in today.
- Are you doing better or worse than yesterday? (Side-by-side comparison.)
- How many orders are live, pending, or delivered.
- Your best-selling products at a glance.
No more calling your manager to ask "how's business." You just look.

2. OMNICHANNEL ORDER MANAGEMENT
Orders come from everywhere: Website, WhatsApp, Facebook, walk-in customers. This system pulls every one into a single, clean board.
- Visual Order Board: Your team drags orders from "Cooking/Packing" to "Ready" to "Delivered." No paper, no confusion.
- Manual Order Entry: Walk-ins or Messenger orders are added in seconds and join the exact same queue as website orders.
- Repeat Buyer Alerts: The system flags VIP customers who have ordered before, so your team can give them special treatment.
- Full Order History: Search any customer's order history in 2 seconds by phone number.

3. ADVANCED PRODUCT & INVENTORY
- Variation Support: Manage sizes (S, M, L, XL) and colors in one interface.
- Combo & Bundle Logic: If you sell a "Family Bundle," the system is smart enough to know it contains 3 separate items. When you sell the bundle, each individual item is automatically subtracted from stock.
- Zero-Floor Safety: The system physically cannot sell a product you don't have. Stock will stop at zero. No negative stock, no liability.
- AI Description Writer: Built-in AI generates professional, sales-focused product descriptions in seconds.
- Multi-language: Bangla, English, and Regional dialects supported for product names and descriptions.

4. CUSTOMER DIRECTORY & CRM
- Every customer's phone number, name, address, and full order history is stored and searchable.
- Loyalty Tracking: See exactly how many times a customer has returned to buy. Reward them appropriately.
- Fake Order & Ban System: Mark orders as "Fake" and permanently ban abusive phone numbers, protecting your delivery budget and your team's time.

5. CATEGORIES & SHOP NAVIGATION
- Your customers navigate your shop exactly how you design it.
- Featured Sections: Highlight "New Arrivals," "Sale Items," or "Seasonal Picks" on the homepage.

=======================================================
SECTION 4: THE ENGINEERING EDGE (WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT)
=======================================================

These are features that require deep software engineering expertise. They cannot be built with a free website builder or an AI tool.

● MULTI-TENANT SAAS ARCHITECTURE
One single platform powers unlimited shops simultaneously. Crab Khai runs on it. Your clothing brand can run on it. Each shop has its own isolated data, security, and settings. This is the foundation of a million-dollar SaaS business.

● RELATIONAL DATA INTEGRITY
When a "Combo" product is sold, the system runs a deep database transaction to find every child component and deduct the correct quantity simultaneously. This is protected by database-level locks to prevent any data corruption during high-traffic sales.

● HARDWARE-LEVEL SECURITY
Trusted Device Registration is a hardware-layer security system. Even if a password is compromised, the system blocks access on any unregistered device. This is the standard in banking software.

● SERVER-SIDE CONVERSION TRACKING (Meta CAPI)
Standard websites track sales in the browser, which gets blocked by 70% of iPhone users and AdBlockers. Our server talks directly to Facebook's servers using an encrypted handshake. Your sales data is captured 100% of the time, resulting in better ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) for your Facebook/Instagram campaigns.

● DOCKER-POWERED SELF-HEALING INFRASTRUCTURE
The system is containerized. If the server crashes for any reason, it detects the failure and restarts itself automatically within seconds — no downtime, no data loss. This is the industry standard for enterprise applications.

● DYNAMIC SEO ENGINE
Built-in automated sitemaps that update every hour. JSON-LD Schema markup that tells Google the exact price, availability, and rating of every product in computer language, resulting in richer Google Search results and more organic traffic.

● MODULAR ARCHITECTURE (BUILT FOR SCALE)
The system is not "locked in." Every feature is modular. Adding a Mobile App, a POS (Point of Sale) machine, or a new payment gateway is a plug-in operation, not a rebuild. This protects your investment for the long term.
